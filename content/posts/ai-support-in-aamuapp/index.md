The broader operation set follows the same OpenAPI-described surface as the public Aamu API, but internal AI actions run directly with the signed-in user's project access rather than an API key.</p><p>This is the product-action side of the same AI system: Team Brain helps Aamu answer from company knowledge, while AI commands help Aamu act on the workspace. Higher-risk actions such as sending, deleting, or running database mutations require explicit intent or confirmation.</p><h2>Ask Aamu for product help</h2><p>Aamu also has a dedicated AI product-help path. In the Launch AI dialog, start a question with <code>aamu:</code> when you want to ask how Aamu.app works rather than perform a workspace action.</p><pre><code class="language-plaintext">aamu: How do I share a Doc with a public link?</code></pre><p>Ask Aamu searches Aamu's public documentation through a separate support Team Brain. It does not use the current team's Team Brain, modify workspace data, create a support ticket, or pretend to hand the conversation to a person. Answers include links to the public sources used as context.</p><p>The same read-only product-help service is available through the public <code>POST https://support.aamu.app/api/ask</code> endpoint.</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/blog/posts/ask-aamu-for-product-help-with-ai/">Read more about Ask Aamu and the public Support API.</a></p><h2>Company Brain: ask across the workspace</h2><p>Company Brain extends Aamu's grounded AI beyond selected support knowledge. Launch AI and <code>@ai</code> can retrieve information from tasks, Docs, meetings and transcripts, forms, file metadata, databases, Helpdesk tickets, the user's own email, newsletters, projects, and team members.</p><p>Retrieval is permission-aware: answers can use company-level information and only the projects available to the signed-in user. User-owned sources such as email have an additional owner check. Answers can include links back to the Aamu objects used as sources.</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/blog/posts/company-brain-in-aamuapp-ask-ai-across-your-workspace/">Read how Company Brain turns workspace data into permission-aware AI answers.</a></p>

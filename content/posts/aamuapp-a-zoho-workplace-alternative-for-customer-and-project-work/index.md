A project can contain Tasks, Docs, email, meetings, Databases and Helpdesk. For example, an onboarding request can remain a customer conversation while an assigned Task tracks the internal change and a Doc holds the maintained instructions.</p><ul><li><p><strong>Helpdesk and Livechat:</strong> handle customer email and website conversations, leave internal comments and create follow-up Tasks from tickets.</p></li><li><p><strong>Tasks and Docs:</strong> assign the work and keep the explanation accessible to the people doing it.</p></li><li><p><strong>Databases and Forms:</strong> maintain structured records and collect responses in the workspace. Database rows can link to Aamu objects such as Docs, Tasks and meetings.</p></li><li><p><strong>Knowledge for AI:</strong> selected Helpdesk Knowledge Base sources can support reply drafts. Writer, Sheet and Show give teams dedicated document, spreadsheet and presentation tools. WorkDrive provides shared file management. That makes Workplace a sensible choice for a team whose daily work centres on email and office files. The <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://www.zoho.com/workplace/">product overview</a> also describes Office-file compatibility and migration options.</p><p>Zoho already connects communication to tasks. Its <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://www.zoho.com/mail/help/adding-tasks.html">email-to-task workflow</a> can create a task from a message and preserve the linked conversation for group work. A useful evaluation should examine the complete customer workflow, including shared support, maintained knowledge and the records behind the request.</p><p>For a dedicated support operation, Zoho offers Desk and documents <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://www.zoho.com/workplace/webinar/workplace-desk-integration.html">Workplace integrations with Desk</a>. Its broader <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://www.zoho.com/one/applications/web.html">Zoho One catalogue</a> includes customer support, CRM, project management and other business applications. Include those products in the evaluation when relevant; Workplace alone and the full Zoho portfolio have different scopes.</p><h2>Compare the whole customer workflow</h2><p>Use a realistic request, such as a customer asking for help with an integration:</p><ol><li><p>Receive the question in Aamu Helpdesk email or Livechat.</p></li><li><p>Find the maintained instructions. If using AI reply drafts, review the retrieved sources and the proposed answer before sending.</p></li><li><p>Create and assign a Task if the request needs a product or process change.</p></li><li><p>Update the relevant Doc and, where needed, a customer or integration record in a Database.</p></li><li><p>Return to the customer conversation with the result. Aamu supports Aamu-hosted domains as well as third-party IMAP/SMTP setups; the domain is attached to a project and users create their project mailboxes. Personal mailboxes and the shared Helpdesk queue serve different workflows.</p><p>If your work depends on complex spreadsheets or exact document formatting, test representative files. Zoho has dedicated office editors. Aamu Databases handle structured records, relations and automation; evaluate your spreadsheet calculations and import/export requirements individually before moving them.</p><p>Zoho's <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://www.zoho.com/workplace/help/ai-in-zoho-workplace.html">AI documentation</a> describes Zia across Workplace applications and bring-your-own-key integrations in supported apps. For either product, check which sources an answer can access and how administrators configure the feature. In Aamu, Helpdesk reply drafts wait for a human to send them; AI Livechat can answer visitors directly when enabled. Company Brain retrieval uses workspace access controls, and private email is scoped to its owner.</p><h2>Pricing: estimate your actual workload</h2><p>Aamu's <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://aamu.app/">published pricing</a> offers a free plan with 25 MB of file storage. Resource billing has a US$1/month minimum plus usage, including storage at US$0.50/GB-month. AI requires your own provider key, with charges paid directly to that provider. Use a small pilot to measure the resources your team needs.</p><p>Zoho's <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://www.zoho.com/workplace/plan-comparison.html">USD plan comparison</a> lists Standard at US$3/user/month annually or US$4 monthly, and Professional at US$6 annually or US$7 monthly. Add relevant products and check local taxes and billing terms. Workplace's <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://www.zoho.com/workplace/pricing.html">pricing page</a> lists Projects and Tables as additional capabilities. Compare the total configuration and capacity you need before drawing a cost conclusion.</p><p>Zoho also advertises a limited free Mail plan in selected data centres, with up to five users and no IMAP, POP or ActiveSync. Its scope differs from the full paid Workplace bundle.</p><h2>Which option fits your team?</h2><p>Choose Zoho Workplace when hosted mail, shared office files and its communication tools cover most of your needs, especially if you already use the wider Zoho ecosystem. Verify the edition for storage, administration and retention requirements.</p><p>Choose Aamu when customer conversations regularly create internal Tasks, documentation changes and Database updates, and you want to handle that work within the same project workspace.

That changes the shape of the workflow.</p><p>A customer message may contain a product question, a complaint, a sales signal, a security concern, a bug report, a missing policy, a request for a human, or several of those at once. The next action might be a reply draft, a task, an assignment, a new Doc, a search for an existing Doc, or a Helpdesk Knowledge Base update.</p><p>Aamu's triage result can therefore include:</p><ul><li><p><strong>Intent</strong>: what the customer is asking.</p></li><li><p><strong>Suggested action</strong>: for example, reply, assign to human, create follow-up work, or search existing Docs.</p></li><li><p><strong>Summary</strong>: what the ticket is about in plain language.</p></li><li><p><strong>Suggested reply</strong>: a draft when it is appropriate to prepare one.</p></li><li><p><strong>Suggested follow-up work</strong>: a task when the answer needs investigation or product input.</p></li><li><p><strong>Risk flags</strong>: reasons the user should not answer too confidently.</p></li><li><p><strong>Relevant knowledge</strong>: what Helpdesk Knowledge Base or Aamu Docs can confirm, and what is missing.</p></li></ul><img src="6b24fea187780851_helpdesk.jpg" alt="Aamu Helpdesk ticket list beside a Livechat conversation" style="width: auto; --upload-progress: 0%;"><p>Helpdesk and Livechat in Aamu’s public product example. <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://aamu.app/blog/posts/aamuapp-helpdesk-feature-overview/">See the complete support workflow.</a></p><h2>What Linear triage is optimized for</h2><p>Linear describes Triage as a special inbox for issues created by integrations, other teams, or people outside the owning team. Before an issue enters the normal workflow, the team can review and decide what should happen to it.</p><p>The core actions are deliberately issue-shaped:</p><ul><li><p><strong>Accept</strong> the issue into the team's workflow.</p></li><li><p><strong>Mark as duplicate</strong> and merge it into an existing canonical issue.</p></li><li><p><strong>Decline</strong> the issue and optionally explain why.</p></li><li><p><strong>Snooze</strong> the issue until later or until there is new activity.</p></li><li><p><strong>Route</strong> the issue to another team, status, assignee, label, project, or priority.</p></li></ul><p>Linear also adds several automation layers around that workflow. Triage Rules apply deterministic actions when issues match configured conditions. Triage Intelligence uses AI to suggest properties and relationships, such as team, project, assignee, labels, related issues, and duplicates. Linear Agent automations can perform more flexible instruction-based actions when issues enter triage.</p><h2>A simple example</h2><p>Imagine a customer writes:</p><blockquote><p>Can Aamu.app be self-hosted?</p></blockquote><p>In Linear, this might become an issue or an Asks request. The triage workflow might route it to the right team, add a customer label, link it to an existing issue about self-hosting, or assign someone to respond.</p><p>In Aamu, the first concern is different. The support team needs a correct customer-facing answer. Self-hosting is not the kind of question AI should improvise from general SaaS assumptions. The answer should come from a maintained policy.</p><p>Aamu triage can say: this is a self-hosting availability question, the answer has security and commercial implications, do not make claims unless policy knowledge confirms them, and look for an existing Doc or Helpdesk Knowledge Base source before drafting a reply.</p><p>If a policy Doc exists but is not yet part of that Helpdesk project's Helpdesk Knowledge Base sources, Aamu can make that visible. The user can add the Doc to Helpdesk Knowledge Base, reindex it, and then ask AI to draft the reply from the newly available source.</p><h2>Side-by-side comparison</h2><table style="min-width: Its <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://linear.app/docs/triage">Triage documentation</a> covers issue review and routing, while <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://linear.app/docs/triage-intelligence">Triage Intelligence</a> addresses suggested properties and relationships. <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://linear.app/docs/linear-agent">Linear Agent documentation</a> describes its agent workflows. Compare the controls that matter to your team: duplicates, routing rules, responsibility and which suggestions can be applied automatically.</p><h2>Where Aamu has a different advantage</h2><p>Aamu's advantage is that its triage can stay close to the customer conversation and the company knowledge needed to answer it.</p><p>A Helpdesk item can lead directly to a reply draft. It can also lead to a task when someone needs to investigate. It can lead to a Doc when the team needs a maintained policy. It can lead to Helpdesk Knowledge Base source updates when the right knowledge exists but is not yet available to AI. It can lead to human assignment when the situation is sensitive.</p><p>This is also why Aamu's human-in-the-loop model matters. Aamu can draft a reply, but sending the reply is a separate explicit action. AI can create or find a Doc, but the team decides whether that Doc should become a Helpdesk knowledge source. AI can suggest assignment, but the user can choose who should own the ticket.</p><h2>How a ticket improves maintained support knowledge</h2><p>The most important Aamu idea is the knowledge loop:</p><ol><li><p>A customer asks a question.</p></li><li><p>AI triage identifies the intent and risk.</p></li><li><p>Helpdesk Knowledge Base retrieves what the company already knows.</p></li><li><p>If the knowledge is missing, AI can suggest a Doc or task.</p></li><li><p>If the knowledge exists but is not connected, the user can add it to Helpdesk Knowledge Base sources.</p></li><li><p>The user can reindex Helpdesk Knowledge Base and ask AI to draft again.</p></li><li><p>A human reviews and sends the final reply.</p></li><li><p>The improved knowledge helps future tickets.</p></li></ol><h2>Before you switch</h2><h3>Does Linear require Business for all triage?</h3><p>Core Triage and Triage Intelligence are different capabilities. The <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://linear.app/pricing">current plan matrix</a> lists Triage Intelligence with Business. Check the controls and integrations you need rather than treating the Business AI price as the entry price for all issue intake.</p><h3>Can Aamu replace our engineering backlog as well?</h3><p>Test issue relationships, duplicate handling, cycles, routing, reporting and code-tool integrations separately. Aamu’s support triage workflow does not by itself establish parity with your engineering planning setup.</p><h3>What should a support triage pilot include?</h3><p>Use a known answer, a missing policy, a duplicate report and a request for a human. Verify retrieved sources, proposed assignments and follow-up Tasks, then review the final customer reply before sending.</p><h2>Test intake with four real requests</h2><p>Choose Linear when engineering issue intake is the central job.

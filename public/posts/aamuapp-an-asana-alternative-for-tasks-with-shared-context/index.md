Its strength is making execution visible.</p><p>When evaluating either tool, follow a task back to its source: a customer request, meeting, document or connected service. Check how easily the team can find and update that context.</p><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://asana.com/features">Asana’s current feature set</a> also covers forms, project views, custom fields, integrations and AI workflows.

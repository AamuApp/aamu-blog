    Aamu.app: a Zoho Workplace alternative for customer and project work

    Looking for a Zoho Workplace alternative? Compare Aamu.app for customer support, Docs, Tasks and Databases, with pricing and migration considerations.
    October 21, 2026 · Updated October 22, 2026 · 7 min · Ilkka Huotari

    Short answer: Aamu.app is a Zoho Workplace alternative for small teams that want customer support, Tasks, Docs, meetings and structured Database records in one project workspace. Zoho Workplace is a strong fit for hosted email and collaborative office files.

    A customer question can turn into a documentation update, a project task and a meeting decision. If your team is evaluating a Zoho Workplace alternative, check how each of those steps stays connected to the original request.

    This comparison is written from Aamu's product perspective, using Aamu's implementation and the official Zoho sources linked below, checked on 21 October 2026.

    Aamu.app: customer and project work in the same workspace

    Aamu is worth evaluating when the same people answer customers, maintain documentation and deliver the follow-up work. A project can contain Tasks, Docs, email, meetings, Databases and Helpdesk. For example, an onboarding request can remain a customer conversation while an assigned Task tracks the internal change and a Doc holds the maintained instructions.

    • Helpdesk and Livechat: handle customer email and website conversations, leave internal comments and create follow-up Tasks from tickets.

    • Tasks and Docs: assign the work and keep the explanation accessible to the people doing it.

    • Databases and Forms: maintain structured records and collect responses in the workspace. Database rows can link to Aamu objects such as Docs, Tasks and meetings.

    • Knowledge for AI: selected Helpdesk Knowledge Base sources can support reply drafts. Company Brain can retrieve accessible workspace information with links back to its sources.

    Aamu Helpdesk and the Livechat widget, shown in the product's public interface example.

    To assess that fit, create an Aamu workspace and try one customer request through to a completed Task and an updated Doc.

    What Zoho Workplace already does well

    Zoho Workplace combines hosted business email, calendars, file storage, office editors, messaging and meetings. Writer, Sheet and Show give teams dedicated document, spreadsheet and presentation tools. WorkDrive provides shared file management. That makes Workplace a sensible choice for a team whose daily work centres on email and office files. The product overview also describes Office-file compatibility and migration options.

    Zoho already connects communication to tasks. Its email-to-task workflow can create a task from a message and preserve the linked conversation for group work. A useful evaluation should examine the complete customer workflow, including shared support, maintained knowledge and the records behind the request.

    For a dedicated support operation, Zoho offers Desk and documents Workplace integrations with Desk. Its broader Zoho One catalogue includes customer support, CRM, project management and other business applications. Include those products in the evaluation when relevant; Workplace alone and the full Zoho portfolio have different scopes.

    Compare the whole customer workflow

    Use a realistic request, such as a customer asking for help with an integration:

    1. Receive the question in Aamu Helpdesk email or Livechat.

    2. Find the maintained instructions. If using AI reply drafts, review the retrieved sources and the proposed answer before sending.

    3. Create and assign a Task if the request needs a product or process change.

    4. Update the relevant Doc and, where needed, a customer or integration record in a Database.

    5. Return to the customer conversation with the result. Reindex the Helpdesk knowledge sources after changing the maintained answer.

    Run the equivalent scenario with your proposed Zoho configuration. Record which apps and subscriptions it uses, where ownership is assigned and how colleagues find the original request. That gives you a concrete basis for choosing between the two setups.

    Email, office files and AI: check the details

    Both products support business email. Zoho Mail provides hosting, custom domains and collaborative mail features. Aamu supports Aamu-hosted domains as well as third-party IMAP/SMTP setups; the domain is attached to a project and users create their project mailboxes. Personal mailboxes and the shared Helpdesk queue serve different workflows.

    If your work depends on complex spreadsheets or exact document formatting, test representative files. Zoho has dedicated office editors. Aamu Databases handle structured records, relations and automation; evaluate your spreadsheet calculations and import/export requirements individually before moving them.

    Zoho's AI documentation describes Zia across Workplace applications and bring-your-own-key integrations in supported apps. For either product, check which sources an answer can access and how administrators configure the feature. In Aamu, Helpdesk reply drafts wait for a human to send them; AI Livechat can answer visitors directly when enabled. Company Brain retrieval uses workspace access controls, and private email is scoped to its owner.

    Pricing: estimate your actual workload

    Aamu's published pricing offers a free plan with 25 MB of file storage. Resource billing has a US$1/month minimum plus usage, including storage at US$0.50/GB-month. AI requires your own provider key, with charges paid directly to that provider. Use a small pilot to measure the resources your team needs.

    Zoho's USD plan comparison lists Standard at US$3/user/month annually or US$4 monthly, and Professional at US$6 annually or US$7 monthly. Add relevant products and check local taxes and billing terms. Workplace's pricing page lists Projects and Tables as additional capabilities. Compare the total configuration and capacity you need before drawing a cost conclusion.

    Zoho also advertises a limited free Mail plan in selected data centres, with up to five users and no IMAP, POP or ActiveSync. Its scope differs from the full paid Workplace bundle.

    Which option fits your team?

    Choose Zoho Workplace when hosted mail, shared office files and its communication tools cover most of your needs, especially if you already use the wider Zoho ecosystem. Verify the edition for storage, administration and retention requirements.

    Choose Aamu when customer conversations regularly create internal Tasks, documentation changes and Database updates, and you want to handle that work within the same project workspace. Start with one project, a few representative files and the people who will actually use the workflow.

    Start with an Aamu workspace and bring one real customer workflow. Check that the team can find the original request, assign the next step and maintain the answer before expanding the move.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Aamu a free Zoho Workplace alternative?

    Aamu has a free plan with 25 MB of file storage, suitable for a small pilot. Resource billing starts at US$1/month plus usage, and AI provider charges are separate. Zoho offers a limited free Mail plan in selected data centres; the full Workplace bundle is paid.

    Does Zoho Workplace include Zoho Desk and Projects?

    Zoho Desk is a separate support product with Workplace integrations. Zoho lists Projects as an additional Workplace capability. Check the products and licences included in your chosen package or quote.

    Can Aamu use email hosted by another provider?

    Yes. Aamu supports third-party IMAP/SMTP setups as well as Aamu-hosted domains. Verify the domain and receiving and sending settings, attach the domain to a project, and test the mailbox before moving production email.

    What should we test before switching from Zoho Workplace?

    Try a complete customer request through support, an assigned Task and an updated Doc. Also check representative office files, Database records and relationships, access permissions, email routing, required integrations and total cost. Move one project first.
